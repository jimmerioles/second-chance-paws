# Design System: Second Chance Paws

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Neighborhood Repertory House"**

Second Chance Paws runs a revival house for puppies. The surface borrows the working parts of a neighborhood cinema — the changeable-letter marquee, the printed showbill, the lobby card, the ticket stub, the box-office ledger — and puts the rescue's facts inside them. It is warm because the photographs are warm, not because the page is beige. It is playful because the type and the bill are playful, not because the page is covered in paw prints.

The material is printed matter under stage light: deep projector green, butter-yellow marquee panels, tomato-red tickets, newsprint stock, and a fine paper grain. Everything is square, ruled, and registered; nothing is rounded or glassy. The one photographic element on screen is the supplied puppy photograph, kept untinted so it remains the only full-colour material in the room.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A full-bleed photograph treated as the screen, with an overlapping butter marquee as the page's first thesis.
- Changeable-letter display type (Alfa Slab One) against a box-office mono (Courier Prime) and a plain program text (Hanken Grotesk).
- Three grounds — projector green, newsprint, butter — sharing one grid and one spacing rhythm.
- Lobby cards, filmstrip reels, showtimes, a concession board, and a program-note dialog as the recurring artifacts.
- Square corners, 1px rules, dashed perforations, registration crosses, and bulb rails in place of rounded containers.

## Colors

A four-role palette with one warm light and one alert channel.

### Primary
- **Ticket Red** (#c8402e): Primary actions, the active filter, the box-office close control, and error text. Darker **Ticket Red Deep** (#9e2f22) is the hover and small-text variant.

### Secondary
- **Marquee Butter** (#f2c14e): The lit marquee, the foster/donate field, and the concession amounts. **Marquee Light** (#f8df9b) is reserved for bulbs and small highlights.

### Tertiary
- **Projector Green** (#0e2f25): Header, process section, dark program surfaces. **Forest Deep** (#09221a) is the filmstrip, footer, and modal backdrop.

### Neutral
- **Newsprint** (#f4eddc): The page's default ground and input fields.
- **Cream** (#fbf6e8): Lobby cards, dialog paper, text on dark grounds.
- **Ink** (#17150f): Body and display text.
- **Muted Ink** (#5f5848): Secondary metadata on newsprint and cream.
- **Error Tint** (#fbe9df): The invalid-field ground; a tint of paper and ticket red, never a new hue.
- **House Black** (#000000): Print stylesheet only; never a screen colour.

### Named Rules

**The House Light Rule.** Butter is the only warm light source. It owns whole surfaces, not scattered accents, and it never tints the photographs.

**The Single Alert Channel Rule.** Red is reserved for action, selection, and error. Status and metadata stay ink-on-ground; if everything is red, nothing is an alert.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Alfa Slab One (with Rockwell, Courier New, serif)
**Body Font:** Hanken Grotesk (with Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** Courier Prime (with Courier New, monospace)

**Character:** A heavy, warm slab for the bill; a humanist grotesque for reading; a typewriter mono for the box office. The pairing sounds like a hand-painted sign, a printed program, and a ticket window in the same building.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400, clamp(40px, 4.85vw, 78px), 0.98): The hero headline and the largest section statements only.
- **Headline** (400, clamp(34px, 4.1vw, 62px), 1.02): Section titles. Never used for body or controls.
- **Title** (400, 22–38px, 1.0–1.05): Puppy names, reel titles, event names, dialog names.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.6): Program copy, bios, and descriptions; measure held to 65–75ch where the layout allows.
- **Label** (400, 0.7rem, 0.08em, uppercase): Box-office labels, dates, tags, counts, and footnotes. Never below 11px.

The frontmatter carries the intervening steps (title XL–S, numeral XL–M, body lead/sm/control/legal, bio, seal) so the system can reproduce the page without one-off sizes.

### Named Rules

**The Changeable-Letter Rule.** Alfa Slab One is the voice of the sign: headlines, names, dates, and amounts. It never sets a paragraph, a label, or a control.

**The Box-Office Rule.** Courier Prime carries measurement and administration — dates, counts, tags, labels, ticket stubs. It is not a decorative "technical" texture and it never replaces body copy.

## Layout

A 76rem maximum program width with fluid gutters (`clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)`) and a single 12-column-feeling rhythm. Sections alternate grounds (green, newsprint, green, newsprint, butter, newsprint) to pace the scroll like a program: dense bill, quiet process, dated showtimes, warm appeal, small box office, dark close.

The hero is a full-bleed screen with a butter marquee overlapping its lower edge; the marquee's top rail carries the now-showing label, and its body is a two-column headline + program note composition, with the supplied location eyebrow heading the program-note column beside the headline. The puppy bill is a 3/2/1 grid of lobby cards. The process is one continuous filmstrip with three reels. Events are a showtime list with a dark date stub. Foster/donate is a split field with a dark concession board. The newsletter is a program page with a ticket-like form. The dialog is a single program-note spread: portrait plate, then the facts.

Breakpoints: 1080px (cards to two columns), 940px (hero, foster, newsletter, and footer to one column), 860px (filter rail and program note), 760px (two-row mobile header; static header), 560px (compact marquee and showtimes). Below 760px the header is static; above it is sticky.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from printed layers and stage light, not from floating cards. Shadows are always offset and soft (`0 24px 45px -30px rgba(9,34,26,0.58)`), used when a surface is physically in front of another: the marquee over the screen, a lobby card under the cursor, the dialog over the house. There are no hard offset shadows, no zero-offset coloured halos, and no glass or blur as decoration. The paper grain is a 120px SVG-noise tile at 0.18 opacity on newsprint sections.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Marquee** (`0 24px 45px -30px rgba(9,34,26,0.58)`): The hero panel over the photograph.
- **Lobby lift** (`0 28px 42px -28px rgba(9,34,26,0.68)`): A card on hover.
- **Dialog** (`0 40px 80px -30px rgba(0,0,0,0.7)`): The program note over the darkened house.
- **Filmstrip inset** (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.22)`): The internal registration edge on the filmstrip.

### Named Rules

**The Lightbox Rule.** A shadow means the object is lit from the front and sitting above the page. If it does not carry that physical story, it does not get a shadow.

## Shapes

Square corners throughout (`0` radius). Borders are 1–2px ink or cream rules, never rounded pill outlines. Recurring silhouettes: the marquee frame with an inset hairline, the lobby card with a cream matte and a 4:5 plate, the ticket stub with a dashed perforation, the filmstrip with sprocket-dot rails, the circular box-office seal, and the registration cross at plate corners. No geometric masks, no blobs, no glass panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** Square (`0`), 1.5px border, minimum height 44px on touch surfaces.
- **Primary:** Ticket red ground, cream text, ticket-red-deep border, soft offset shadow; hovers to ticket-red-deep and lifts 2px.
- **Outline:** Transparent ground, ink border and text on light grounds; cream border and text on forest. Hover inverts.
- **Header ticket:** Smaller red button with a butter border; hover flips to butter ground and ink text.

### Filter Chips
- **Style:** Transparent ground, ink hairline, Courier Prime uppercase; selected state is ticket-red ground with cream text and a small butter dot.
- **State:** `aria-pressed="true"` is the only selected state. No rounded pills.

### Lobby Cards
- **Corner Style:** Square.
- **Background:** Cream matte, 1px ink-tinted border, 4:5 photograph with a 1px inner rule.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Lightbox lift on hover/focus.
- **Border:** 1px ink at 22% for the card; 1px ink at 16% for the plate.
- **Internal Padding:** 0.6rem around the plate; 1–1.15rem in the body.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Newsprint ground, 1px ink-tinted border, square, 51px minimum height.
- **Focus:** 3px ticket-red outline with 2px offset.
- **Error:** Ticket-red-deep border, a pale red-tinted ground, and inline text linked by `aria-describedby`.

### Navigation
- **Style:** Sticky forest bar above 760px, static two-row bar below. Brand is a red SCP ticket mark plus Alfa Slab One; links are Courier Prime uppercase at 0.7rem with a butter underline on hover; Donate is a red header ticket. All controls keep a 44px minimum touch height.

### Signature Components
- **Marquee:** Butter panel with a forest top rail, bulb rail, and inset hairline; the hero's single dominant move.
- **Filmstrip:** Forest-deep strip with top and bottom sprocket-dot rails, three reels divided by 1px cream rules.
- **Concession Board:** Forest-deep panel with a butter inset rule, Alfa Slab amounts, dotted leaders, and Courier Prime impact labels.
- **Program Note:** The modal spread; newsprint paper, red close tab, circular seal over the portrait plate, dashed fact rules, and a red adoption ticket.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the three grounds distinct and whole; a butter surface is a field, not an accent.
- **Do** use Alfa Slab One for the bill, the name, the date, and the amount; use Courier Prime for measurement and labels.
- **Do** keep photographs untinted and let them be the only full-colour material.
- **Do** hold every functional label at 11px or larger, and every touch control at 44px or larger.
- **Do** use square corners, 1px rules, dashes, and registration marks to carry the printed world.
- **Do** reserve red for action, selection, and error.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add rounded cards, pill chips, glass, blur, or gradient text.
- **Don't** use hard offset shadows outside a physical lightbox story.
- **Don't** use red for decoration or status; it stops meaning "act" or "wrong".
- **Don't** set body copy in the display slab or in the mono.
- **Don't** put a generic eyebrow over a heading; the supplied eyebrows live in rails, tabs, or beside the program copy.
- **Don't** invent testimonials, partners, or claims. The supplied photographs and facts are the only evidence.
