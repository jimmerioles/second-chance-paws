How it works

From application to homecoming.

No auctions, no guesswork. Just a thoughtful match for both of you.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Tell us about your home, your schedule, and the kind of puppy you're hoping for. It takes about 10 minutes.

  2. 02

    Meet

    We'll set up a low-pressure meet at the puppy's foster home or at a weekend social.

  3. 03

    Bring them home

    You'll go home with food, a favorite blanket, and a 30-day check-in from our team.
